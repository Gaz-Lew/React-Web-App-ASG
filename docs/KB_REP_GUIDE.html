What is ASG CRM?

ASG CRM is the central tool your team uses to manage every lead — from the moment it's created to the point a deal is settled. Everything lives here: lead details, call history, notes, appointments, and your daily task list.

You do not need to be technical to use it. This guide covers everything you need to do your job well.

Your Daily Workflow

Follow this sequence every working day. It keeps your pipeline clean and makes sure nothing slips through.

  1. Open the Dashboard
    The dashboard is your home base. It shows today's call count, callback reminders, and your priority work queue — the leads that need attention right now.
  2. Check the Priority Work Queue
    Scroll down on the dashboard to the Priority Work Queue section. These are leads flagged as needing action today, sorted from most urgent to least. Work through them in order.
    • 🔴 Red = High priority — act immediately
    • 🟡 Amber = Medium priority — do today
    • ⚪ Grey = Low priority — when time allows
  3. Work your leads
    Click any lead to open the Lead Detail panel. From here you can:
    • See the lead's full info (name, phone, status, history)
    • Call them using the Log Call button
    • Add a note
    • Change their status
  4. Log every contact
    Every time you speak to a lead — or try to — log it. This keeps the system accurate and means your manager can support you properly.
  5. Book appointments through the Calendar
    If a lead is ready to meet, go to the Calendar page and book the appointment. Link it to the lead so everything stays connected.
  6. End of day — check callbacks
    Review your callback queue on the dashboard. If any are overdue, handle them first thing tomorrow.

How to update a lead

  1. Click the lead's row in the Leads table to open the sidebar
  2. Change the status using the Lead Status dropdown
  3. The system saves automatically after a short pause — you'll see a toast confirmation
  4. You can also manually click Save Changes at any time

Using the Notes field vs. Note History

  • Notes field (top of the form) — a quick summary about the lead. Use this for key context that should always be visible at a glance. Example: "Husband is the decision maker, call after 5pm"
  • Note History (further down the sidebar) — a timestamped log of individual notes added over time. Use this to record what happened on each contact. Example: "Called Tuesday — interested but wants to speak to partner first"

Calendar & Appointments

How bookings work

  1. Go to the Calendar page
  2. Click the + New Appointment button
  3. Fill in the client name, date, time, rep, and service type
  4. If the lead is in the system, link it using the Linked Lead field
  5. Save — the appointment will appear in the day and week views

Important: If a lead no-shows or cancels, the system will flag that lead in the Priority Work Queue automatically with a "Reschedule" action. Follow up and rebook as soon as possible.

Notes & Call History

Why notes matter

Notes are your memory. When you have 50+ leads, you cannot remember every conversation. Good notes mean that when you pick up the phone to call someone back, you know exactly where you left off — and the lead feels heard.

Notes also mean that if another rep needs to cover for you, they can step in without the lead having to repeat themselves.

Best practices for notes

  • Add a note immediately after every call — while it's fresh
  • Include the outcome: what they said, what they need, and what the next step is
  • Keep it brief but complete — two to three sentences is usually enough
  • Don't write vague notes like "called" — write what actually happened

Good note example

"Spoke to Sarah. She's interested but said her husband hasn't seen it yet. She'll call back Thursday. Very warm — follow up if she doesn't call."

Bad note example

"Called. No answer."

(If there was no answer, use the call logger and select No Answer — that is already logged automatically.)

Best Practices

Stay on top of your queue

  • Check the dashboard every morning — the Priority Work Queue tells you exactly where to start
  • Work through high-priority items first, before anything else
  • Never let a callback go overdue — set a reminder and honour it

Keep lead data clean

  • Always update the status after every interaction — never leave a lead stuck in the wrong status
  • If a number is wrong, mark it as Wrong Number immediately so no-one else wastes time
  • If a lead asks not to be called, mark them Not Interested right away

Use the call logger, not just notes

  • The call logger records a structured entry with the date, time, rep name, and result
  • This is what drives the dashboard stats — if you don't log it, it doesn't count

Maximise conversions

  • Speed matters — the faster you call a DQ lead after it's created, the higher the chance of booking
  • Revisit leads are warm — they said yes to a call-back, so treat them as interested
  • Always have a clear next step — before you end every call, confirm what happens next and log it
